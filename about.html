About Grace Connections

Grace Connections provides compassionate, dependable non-medical private-duty care for families who need trusted support at home.

Rooted in strength, grace, and accountability. Established in 2008 and officially launched as an agency in March 2024.

A Faith-Based Care Journey

Grace Connections began with a heart for serving families through practical support, prayerful encouragement, and dependable help at home. The agency exists to help seniors, adults, children, individuals with disabilities, and family caregivers feel less alone in the care journey.

The work is private-duty and non-medical. That means the team focuses on personal support, companion care, activities of daily living, light housekeeping, meals, errands, transportation, respite, and family relief. Emergency medical needs should always be directed to 911.

Care Standards

  • 100% background checks for care associates
  • Zero-tolerance expectations for abuse or neglect
  • Professional communication with families
  • Respect for privacy, dignity, and home routines

Guiding Principles

Grace Connections is built around the values families should expect from anyone entering their home.

Dignity

Support is planned around the person's preferences, privacy, history, and home environment.

Moral Integrity

Faith-based values guide communication, conduct, and care decisions.

Empathy

Every interaction should feel patient, calm, respectful, and family-centered.
