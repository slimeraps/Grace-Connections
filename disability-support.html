Disability Support Care in Illinois

Respectful non-medical care for individuals with disabilities who need dependable personal support, routine assistance, companionship, and family relief.

Care built around ability.Support is planned with attention to independence, safety, dignity, and household preferences.

Dependable Daily Support

Grace Connections provides private-duty assistance for adults, children, and families managing disability-related care routines. Services do not replace medical care, therapy, or clinical treatment.

  • Companionship and supervision
  • Hygiene, dressing, meals, and home routines
  • Mobility support and transportation
  • Respite for parents and family caregivers
  • Virtual caregiver support and practical check-ins

Family-Centered Planning

Families can share routines, communication preferences, sensory considerations, pet needs, and safety concerns during consultation.
