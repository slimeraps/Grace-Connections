Support That Protects Dignity and Independence

Grace Connections helps older adults stay supported at home while giving families a dependable partner. Care is non-medical and focused on practical daily assistance, companionship, and household routines.

  • Companion care and meaningful conversation
  • Bathing, grooming, dressing, and hygiene reminders
  • Mobility support, transfers, and fall-aware routines
  • Meal preparation, hydration prompts, and grocery help
  • Transportation, errands, appointments, and church or social outings
